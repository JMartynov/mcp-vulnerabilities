Scope & Objective:

Instructions:
1. In `src/mcp_vulnerabilities/cli.py`:
   - Add `--sandbox` (choices: `docker`, `subprocess`, `none`, default: `none`) and `--canary-detection` flags to `proxy` subparser.

   - Add tests verifying sandbox driver selection and canary secret leak interception.

Non-Interactive Completion Directive:
